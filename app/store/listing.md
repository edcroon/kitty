## Description
Kitty keeps track of shared costs on trips, nights out and in shared flats, so nobody has to do the maths.

Add what you paid and who it was for, and Kitty works out who owes who. When it's time to square up, it tells you the fewest payments to get everyone even.

- Start a group in seconds and invite friends with a link or QR code
- Split equally, by amounts or by shares
- Spend in any currency - Kitty converts at today's rate
- Tap any total to see it in your own currency
- See where the money went, by day and by type
- Mark payments as paid and watch the balance go to zero
- Works offline and syncs when you're back online

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
